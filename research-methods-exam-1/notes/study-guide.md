# CCJ 4700: Research Methods - Exam 1 Study Guide 🍢

## Ch. 1: Criminal Justice & Scientific Inquiry
### 4 Ways of Knowing
1. **Authority**: Believing someone because they are an expert/boss.
2. **Tradition**: Believing things because "that's the way it's always been."
3. **Personal Experience**: Knowing because you saw it or lived it.
4. **Scientific Approach**: The "Gold Standard." Based on **Empirical Knowledge** (stuff we can measure).

### 4 Research Purposes
- **Exploration**: Exploring a new or "unknown" topic.
- **Description**: Describing the "what" (Scope of the problem).
- **Explanation**: Explaining the "why" (Causes).
- **Applied**: Evaluation (Does it work?) or Problem Analysis.

## Ch. 2: Foundations and Theory
- **Theory**: A logical explanation for patterns we see in life.
- **Hypothesis**: A specific, testable expectation derived from a theory.
- **Variable**: A logical grouping of attributes (e.g., Occupation).
- **Attributes**: Characteristics or qualities that describe an object (e.g., Lawyer, Teacher).
- **Independent Variable (IV)**: The **CAUSE** (The thing that influences change).
- **Dependent Variable (DV)**: The **EFFECT** (The result you are measuring).
- **Control Variable**: Variables held constant to make sure the IV is actually the thing causing the change.
