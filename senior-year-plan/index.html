🌴 Part 2: Regional “Hot Spots” (Southeast Focus)

Kylie is in a prime location. The Southeast has the highest concentration of high-paying/low-cost-of-living (LCOL) forensic roles.

  1. Tampa/St. Pete, FL: High demand due to HCSO and Pinellas labs. High LCOL, but great networking.
  2. Orlando, FL: Huge growth in private forensics and digital evidence labs.
  3. Tallahassee, FL: FDLE Headquarters. If she wants to stay at FSU, this is the hub.
  4. Atlanta/Savannah, GA: GBI (Georgia Bureau of Investigation) pays well and GA has many LCOL pockets.
  5. Charlotte/Raleigh, NC: Booming tech-forensics scene.
