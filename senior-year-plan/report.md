## 📅 Part 1: The Timeline (Senior Year Milestone Plan)

### Phase 0: The "Crucial Summer" (Now - August 2026)
**Goal: Real-World Experience & Security Clearance Prep**
- **Internship Priority:** Target civilian roles in the Tampa Bay area.

- **Skill Building:** Take an online course in **GIS (Geographic Information Systems)**. This is the secret weapon for Crime Analysts.

### Phase 1: Fall Semester (August - December 2026)
**Goal: Network Building & Specialized Certification**
- **FSU Resources:** Meet with the FSU Criminology Internship Coordinator specifically for "Civilian Track" opportunities.
- **Join the IAI:** Join the **International Association for Identification (IAI)** as a student member. It’s the gold standard for Forensics.
- **LinkedIn Optimization:** Overhaul her profile to reflect "Aspiring Forensic Analyst" rather than "Student."

### Phase 2: Winter Break (December 2026 - January 2027)
**Goal: The "AI Agent" Deployment**
- **Automated Search:** Set up the AI agent (via OpenClaw) to scrape USAJobs, FDLE, and private lab boards daily.
- **Resume Tailoring:** Create three specialized versions of her resume:
  1. Forensic/CSI Focused
  2. Intelligence/Crime Analysis Focused
  3. Victim Advocacy/Social Work Focused

### Phase 3: Spring Semester - "The Blitz" (January - April 2027)
**Goal: Mass Application & Interviewing**

## 🌴 Part 2: Regional "Hot Spots" (Southeast Focus)

Kylie is in a prime location. The Southeast has the highest concentration of high-paying/low-cost-of-living (LCOL) forensic roles.

1.  **Tampa/St. Pete, FL:** High demand due to HCSO and Pinellas labs. High LCOL, but great networking.
2.  **Orlando, FL:** Huge growth in private forensics and digital evidence labs.
3.  **Tallahassee, FL:** FDLE Headquarters. If she wants to stay at FSU, this is the hub.
4.  **Atlanta/Savannah, GA:** GBI (Georgia Bureau of Investigation) pays well and GA has many LCOL pockets.
5.  **Charlotte/Raleigh, NC:** Booming tech-forensics scene.
