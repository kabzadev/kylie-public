🩸 HOMICIDE TYPES

Excusable
Accidental kill, NO negligence/recklessness
Justifiable
Self-defense or defense of another
1st Degree Murder
Premeditated + deliberated (planned!)
2nd Degree Murder
Intentional but NOT premeditated (spontaneous)
Felony Murder
Kill during felony commission (e.g., robbery)
Manslaughter
Kill without malice, less responsibility

🏘️ SOCIOLOGICAL THEORIES

Can't achieve goals + remove positives + add negatives = strain → crime
Social Disorganization
Poverty + turnover + family breakdown = weak community bonds
Social Learning
Violence learned through observation + reinforcement
Self-Control Theory
Impulsivity set by age 8, predicts violence
