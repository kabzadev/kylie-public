## 🧠 The "Big Three" Theories
*Pick the theory based on the story scenario.*

### 1. Social Learning Theory (The "David" Story)
- **The Story:** David saw his dad hit his mom, so he hits his partner.
- **The Vibe:** "Monkey see, monkey do."
- **Keywords:** Imitation, Observation, "How I was raised."

### 2. General Strain Theory (The "Jacob" Story)
- **The Story:** Jacob lost his job, got **ANGRY**, and stole a credit card.
- **The Vibe:** "Life is hard and I'm mad."
- **Keywords:** Negative stimuli (losing job), **ANGER** (main emotion), Delinquency.

### 3. Social Disorganization Theory
- **The Story:** A neighborhood where everyone moves every 6 months.
- **The Vibe:** "Nobody knows their neighbor."
- **Keywords:** Residential Mobility, Poverty, Lack of social control.

## ⚖️ Court & Legal Logic
- **Family Court:** Single judge.
- **Civil Court:** Burden is "Probable Cause" / Financial restitution.
- **Criminal Court:** Burden is "Beyond a Reasonable Doubt."
- **Aggravated Assault:** Weapon or serious injury.
- **Simple Assault:** No weapon / minor injury.
- **1st Degree Murder:** Premeditation (Prior knowledge) + Deliberation (Planning).
- **2nd Degree Murder:** Spontaneous but intended (Like the Jorge Torres suitcase case).
- **Manslaughter:** No malice/premeditation ("I messed up").

## 👵 Elder Abuse Facts
- **Most Common Perpetrators:** Family members (Children first, then spouses).
- **Social Support:** The strongest protective factor.
- **COVID-19 Impact:** 83% increase in abuse due to stress/isolation.
- **Financial Exploitation:** Main perpetrators are adult children.
- **Self-Neglect:** Caregivers can be charged even if the elder refuses help.
